Algorithm Analysis

Performance on Large-Scale Problems

On large-scale problems, the algorithms show different characteristics:

  • MCAA: Fast execution but may get stuck in local optima on complex problems
  • GA: Good exploration but requires many generations for large search spaces
  • SA: Good balance of exploration and exploitation with proper cooling schedule
  • PSO: Fast convergence but may converge prematurely on large problems

Conclusions

Based on the large-scale testing:

  • MCAA achieved the highest average score across all scenarios.
  • Algorithm selection becomes more critical as problem size increases.
  • Execution time increases significantly with problem size - plan accordingly.
  • For real-time applications with large problems, consider hybrid approaches.
